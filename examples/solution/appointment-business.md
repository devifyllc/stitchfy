# Project: Riverside Wellness Studio

## Business
- **Business Name:** Riverside Wellness Studio
- **Industry:** Service business (appointments)
- **Description:** A small service business that currently takes appointment requests by phone and WhatsApp for in-person sessions.

## Goals
- Reduce the time employees spend manually scheduling appointments
- Offer booking outside of normal operating hours
- Reduce scheduling conflicts

## Users
- Customers booking appointments
- Front-desk employee handling requests

## Business Processes

Appointment Booking

Trigger:
Customer contacts the business to request an appointment

Actors:
Customer
Front-desk employee

Current systems:
WhatsApp
Google Calendar

Steps:
1. Customer contacts business by phone or WhatsApp
2. Employee checks Google Calendar for availability
3. Employee manually confirms availability with the customer
4. Employee records customer information
5. Employee manually sends a reminder before the appointment

Pain points:
- Appointments can only be handled during operating hours
- Calendar entry is manual and error-prone
- Occasional scheduling conflicts
- Reminders consume employee time

Business rules:
- Appointments cannot overlap for the same employee

Automation candidates:
- Online appointment requests
- Calendar synchronization
- Automated confirmations and reminders

## Pain Points
- Appointments can only be handled during business hours
- Manual calendar entry causes occasional conflicts
- Sending reminders manually takes staff time

## Existing Systems
- Google Calendar
- WhatsApp
- Informational website (no booking capability)

## Integrations
- Google Calendar sync
- WhatsApp notifications

## Desired Outcomes
- Customers can request appointments online at any time
- Calendar stays synchronized automatically
- Confirmations are sent automatically
- An employee approves any request that conflicts with existing bookings
