# Project: Customer Intake Modernization Assessment

## Business
- **Business Name:** Customer Intake Modernization Assessment
- **Industry:** Professional services
- **Description:** A small business relies on a spreadsheet for customer intake and a desktop application for scheduling, with manual file exchange between the two.

## Goals
- Understand whether the current customer intake and scheduling process should be modernized

## Users
- Front-desk staff

## Existing Systems
- **Customer Intake Spreadsheet:** Used to record new customer information.
- **Scheduling Desktop Application:** Used to schedule appointments for customers.

## Modernization Requirements
- The business wants the Customer Intake Spreadsheet and the Scheduling Desktop Application assessed for modernization.
- The Customer Intake Spreadsheet and the Scheduling Desktop Application are connected only by manual file exchange between staff.
- The business reports duplicate manual entry, difficult support, and no reliable audit trail across the Customer Intake Spreadsheet and the Scheduling Desktop Application.
- No replacement technology has been selected.
- No cloud requirement exists.
- No migration strategy has been selected.

## Desired Outcomes
- A clear understanding of whether and how the Customer Intake Spreadsheet and the Scheduling Desktop Application should be modernized
